1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 Skootlink (Pty) Ltd ("Skootlink," "we," "us," or "our"), a company registered in the Republic of South Africa with registration number K2026386113, is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is processed in a lawful, transparent, and secure manner.

1.2 This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect your personal information when you access or use the Skootlink mobile application, website, and associated services (collectively, the "Platform"). It also describes your rights regarding your personal information and how you can exercise them.

1.3 Skootlink is a "responsible party" as defined in the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA"). We determine the purpose and means of processing personal information collected through the Platform. Where we use third-party service providers to process personal information on our behalf, those providers act as our "operators."

1.4 By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any provision of this Privacy Policy, you must not use the Platform.

2. SCOPE OF THIS POLICY

2.1 This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • (a) All users of the Platform, including both "Drivers" (users seeking to rent vehicles or provide delivery services) and "Owners" (users listing vehicles for rent);
  • (b) Visitors to our website who do not register an account;
  • (c) Any other individual whose personal information we may process in the course of operating the Platform.

2.2 This Privacy Policy does not apply to:

  • (a) Personal information processed by other users of the Platform (e.g., information shared between Drivers and Owners). Skootlink is not responsible for how users process each other's personal information outside the Platform;
  • (b) Third-party websites, applications, or services that may be linked to or accessible through the Platform, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

3. DEFINITIONS

3.1 In this Privacy Policy, the following terms shall have the meanings given to them in POPIA, unless the context indicates otherwise:

  • "Personal Information" means any information relating to an identifiable, living natural person or juristic person, including but not limited to contact details, identity numbers, financial information, and location data.
  • "Processing" means any operation or activity concerning personal information, including collection, receipt, recording, organisation, storage, updating, modification, retrieval, consultation, use, dissemination, distribution, merging, linking, blocking, degradation, erasure, or destruction.
  • "Data Subject" means the person to whom personal information relates.
  • "Information Officer" means the person designated by Skootlink to be responsible for compliance with POPIA.
  • "Special Personal Information" means personal information concerning a person's religious or philosophical beliefs, race or ethnic origin, trade union membership, political persuasion, health, sex life, biometric information, or criminal behaviour.

4. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

4.1 Skootlink collects personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, information automatically collected through your use of the Platform, and information obtained from third-party sources. We only collect personal information that is adequate, relevant, and not excessive for the purposes for which it is processed.

4.2 Personal Information You Provide to Us

(a) Account and Registration Data
When you create an account on the Platform, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Password (encrypted)
  • Account type (Driver or Owner)
  • Mobile phone number

(b) Identity Verification Data
When you subscribe and undergo identity verification through our third-party verification provider, we collect and transmit the following for processing:

  • South African ID number or passport number (for foreign nationals)
  • A photograph or scan of your identity document or passport
  • For Drivers: A photograph or scan of your driver's licence
  • Facial biometric data (where consent is provided for selfie matching)
  • Date of birth
  • Nationality

(c) Vehicle Listing Data (Owners)
When you list a vehicle on the Platform, we collect:

  • Vehicle make, model, year, and registration number
  • Vehicle identification number (VIN)
  • Vehicle photographs
  • Rental rates and availability dates
  • Vehicle location
  • Insurance details

(d) Communications Data
When you communicate with us (via email, in-app chat, telephone, or social media), communicate with other users through the Platform, participate in user research or surveys, or submit a complaint or support request, we collect the content of your communication, the date and time, and any attachments or information you choose to provide.

4.3 Information Automatically Collected

(a) Usage Data
We collect information about your interactions with the Platform, including pages or screens viewed, searches conducted, listings created or viewed, bookings made, login history, time spent on pages or screens, navigation paths, and the date and time of your visits.

(b) Device and Technical Data
We collect information about the device you use to access the Platform, including device type, operating system and version, unique device identifiers (such as IDFA or AAID), mobile network information, browser type and version, IP address, and, where you grant permission, approximate or precise location data.

(c) Location Data
With your consent, we may collect precise geolocation data from your mobile device to enable features such as searching for nearby vehicles or tracking vehicle location during an active rental. You may withdraw this consent at any time through your device settings or the Platform's privacy controls.

(d) Cookies and Similar Technologies
We use cookies and similar tracking technologies (such as pixels, web beacons, and local storage) to enhance your experience on the Platform. Cookies are small data files placed on your device. We use:

  • Essential Cookies — Necessary for the Platform to function (e.g., maintaining your login session).
  • Performance Cookies — To analyse how users interact with the Platform and improve its performance.
  • Functional Cookies — To remember your preferences and personalise your experience.
  • Analytics Cookies — To understand usage patterns and optimise the Platform.

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Please see our Cookies Policy for detailed information on the specific cookies we use.

4.4 Information from Third-Party Sources
We may receive personal information about you from:

  • Verification Providers: Confirmation of identity verification status and, where applicable, driving licence validity from Verifynow.
  • Payment Processors: Confirmation of payment transactions from Payfast.
  • Other Users: Information provided by other users in connection with a transaction, complaint, or review.
  • Publicly Available Sources: Information from public registers or databases, where lawful and necessary.

5. LAWFUL BASES FOR PROCESSING YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION

5.1 In accordance with POPIA, we process your personal information only where one or more of the following lawful bases apply:

  • (a) Your Consent: You have given us explicit, informed consent to process your personal information for a specific purpose (e.g., processing sensitive biometric data for identity verification, sending direct marketing communications, or collecting precise geolocation data).
  • (b) Contractual Necessity: The processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which you are a party (i.e., our Terms of Service), or to take steps at your request prior to entering into a contract.
  • (c) Legal Obligation: The processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which Skootlink is subject (e.g., tax laws, anti-money laundering regulations, or responding to lawful requests from regulatory authorities).
  • (d) Legitimate Interests: The processing is necessary for the purposes of Skootlink's legitimate interests or those of a third party, except where such interests are overridden by your interests or fundamental rights and freedoms (e.g., monitoring and improving Platform security, detecting and preventing fraud, and analysing aggregated usage trends).
  • (e) Protection of Legitimate Interests: The processing is necessary to protect a legitimate interest of yours.

5.2 Where we rely on consent as the lawful basis for processing, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. To withdraw consent, please contact us using the details provided in clause 13.

9. DATA SECURITY

9.1 Skootlink takes the security of your personal information seriously and implements reasonable and appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or disclosure. These measures include, but are not limited to:

  • (a) Encryption: All personal information transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS) technology. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Payment card data is encrypted by Payfast and is not accessible to Skootlink.
  • (b) Access Controls: Access to personal information is restricted to authorised employees, contractors, and service providers who have a legitimate business need to access it. Access rights are reviewed periodically and revoked when no longer required.
  • (c) Data Minimisation: We only collect and retain personal information that is necessary for the purposes for which it is processed.
  • (d) Regular Security Assessments: We conduct periodic security reviews and vulnerability assessments of our systems and processes.
  • (e) Incident Response: We maintain an incident response plan to address data security incidents promptly and effectively.

9.2 Despite our security measures, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is entirely secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. In the unfortunate event of a data breach involving your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required by POPIA and the 2025 POPIA Regulations.

11. YOUR RIGHTS UNDER POPIA

11.1 POPIA grants you certain rights regarding your personal information. Skootlink is committed to honouring these rights. You have the right to:

  • (a) Access: Request confirmation of whether we hold personal information about you, and to request a copy or description of such personal information. You may access and update certain personal information directly through your account settings.
  • (b) Correction or Deletion: Request that we correct, update, or delete your personal information if it is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading, or obtained unlawfully. In accordance with the 2025 POPIA Regulations, you may make this request through various channels, including email, at no cost to you. We will notify you of the action taken as a result of your request.
  • (c) Objection: Object to the processing of your personal information, at no cost to you, where such processing is based on our legitimate interests, is for direct marketing purposes, or is otherwise not in compliance with POPIA. In accordance with the 2025 POPIA Regulations, objections may be submitted through various convenient channels, including email and WhatsApp. We must clearly communicate to you your right to object.
  • (d) Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent previously given for the processing of your personal information, including consent for direct marketing, biometric data processing, and location data collection. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to the withdrawal.
  • (e) Complaints: Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe that we have interfered with the protection of your personal information. Under the 2025 POPIA Regulations, complaints may be submitted online, by email, or in person, and in your preferred language where reasonably possible. Your identity may be kept confidential in sensitive cases.
